Check a topic

Open a topic and select House Style > Lint Topic. With text selected, House Style checks the paragraphs that contain it. With nothing selected, it checks the whole topic.

The review pane opens. Suggestions from the style rules appear at once. AI suggestions follow, paragraph by paragraph. With No AI chosen in Settings, the pane says "AI review is off" and shows only the style rules.

Read a suggestion

Suggestions are listed in reading order, under the topic's headings. Select one to see:

  • What changes: the words around it, with removed words struck through and added words underlined.
  • Why: the reason, the rule, and the rule's example if it has one.
  • Source: Google, Our rules, This project, or AI.

For text in a snippet, the pane also says how many files use the snippet.

Decide

ButtonWhat happens
Apply (Enter)Writes the suggestion as a tracked change and goes to the next one. For a suggestion that needs a rewrite, the button says Rewrite….
Reject (Delete)Hides the suggestion for that text. Choose why: wrong, fine here, or the rule doesn't apply. Your reason helps tune the style guide.
Edit…Type the replacement yourself.
Refine…Tell the AI how to change this one suggestion, for example "keep the term SSO".
Disable Rule…Turns the rule off or changes its level, for the team or for this project. If you can't change your team's guide, the button says Request Rule Change…. See Ask for a rule change.
Apply all fixesApplies every exact fix in the topic. One Ctrl+Z undoes them.
Write all as tracked changesWrites every suggestion that has exact text, so you can review them in Flare's Review tab.

Some suggestions need you, such as a missing alt text. Select Go to, fix it in the editor, and then select Check this paragraph again. For a missing alt text, Suggest alt text… asks the AI to draft it.

The AI doesn't change must, should, may or code terms on its own. A suggestion that would change them is left for you to decide.

Undo and check again

If you undo a fix with Ctrl+Z, or reject it in the Review tab, the suggestion comes back in the pane.

Lint Again runs the style rules again and asks the AI for a new review of each paragraph. House Style saves AI reviews, so when you open a topic again, a paragraph you haven't changed shows its saved AI suggestions, at no cost. When you apply a fix, Lint Again keeps the rest of that paragraph's AI suggestions instead of asking the AI again. After Stop, Lint Again reviews only the paragraphs that are left.
