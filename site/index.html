What House Style does

  1. Lint a topic or a whole project

    Lint Project groups the issues by rule, most frequent first. Change All fixes a whole rule in one step.

  2. Your team's rules, with owners

    Your team keeps its rules in a shared Flare project, and they win over Google's. Owners decide on changes, and other writers send a request.

  3. AI only when you ask

    Linting never uses AI. Rewrite and Ask AI use your own Anthropic key or your company's AI gateway, and each action costs a few cents.

  4. Updates itself

    Once a day House Style checks GitHub for a new version. It installs when you close Flare, with no administrator prompt.

    You can turn updates off in Settings, and IT can turn them off for everyone.

  5. Private by design

    Linting runs on your computer and sends nothing. AI actions send the selected text and the rules that apply, only to the provider you choose.
