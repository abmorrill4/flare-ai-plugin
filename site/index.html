How it works

Click Check style on the House Style tab. Style rules run on your computer in seconds. An AI review of the wording is optional.

Every fix shows its reason

A pane beside your topic lists each suggested fix under the topic's headings.

What changes

Removed text is struck through and added text is underlined.

Why

Each fix names its rule and source: Google, your team's style guide, or the AI review.

You decide

Accept a fix, reject it, or edit the wording first.

Your markup is safe

House Style blocks any AI edit that would break a variable, cross-reference, snippet or condition.

Add your team's rules

Avoidsign onUsesign in

Write your rules as tables in a Flare project. Each row becomes a one-click fix, and your rules override Google's.

Keep the project in a shared folder so every writer uses the same rules. Owners change them; other writers send requests. Build style guide suggests rules from your topics or a Word file.

What leaves your computer

Nothing, unless you ask for an AI action.

  • Style checks run on your computer.
  • An AI action sends the paragraphs being checked and the rules that apply. They go to Anthropic under your API key, or to your company's AI service.
  • House Style sends nothing to its authors.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. House Style is open source under the MIT license. AI features are optional and bill your Anthropic account or your company's AI service.

Does my text leave my computer?

Only when you use an AI feature. Privacy lists exactly what is sent.

Which Flare versions does it work with?

Flare 2026 on Windows, in Ribbon UI mode. Flare 2024 and 2025 aren't tested yet. See the version table.

How do I install it?

Extract the download, close Flare, and double-click Install.cmd. Then enable House Style in File > Options > Plugins. The install guide has the details.
