Privacy

Linting runs on your computer. Your text leaves it only when you use an AI action.

Summary

Stays on your computer

  • Your topics
  • Style guides
  • The linter
  • Your key, encrypted
  • Logs
  • Your decisions

Leaves it

  • When you use AISelected text and its rulesTo Anthropic or your company's gateway
  • Once a dayA version checkTo GitHub, with no text or key
  • When you send oneA problem reportTo the authors, with no topic text

AI actions

Rewrite, Ask AI, AI review, Topic Metadata, alt text and the AI steps for style guide owners send the selected text and the rules that apply. Alt text sends the image. Linting never uses AI.

AI settingRequests go to
No AINowhere
My Anthropic API keyThe Anthropic API, under your key
My company's AI serviceYour company's gateway, under its key

Your agreement with Anthropic, or your company's policy, covers how the provider handles your text.

Update check

Once a day House Style asks api.github.com for the latest release. The request has no text, key or ID. GitHub sees your IP address.

A new version is checked against its published SHA-256 checksum before it installs. Offline, the check is skipped.

To turn it off, open Settings, select Advanced and clear Keep House Style up to date. IT can turn it off for everyone with update-policy.json or Install.ps1 -NoAutoUpdate.

Problem reports

House Style collects no usage data. When an error happens, it asks before it sends a report and shows what the report contains. You can also turn on Send error reports automatically in Settings.

A report has version numbers, the error, recent log lines and your settings without your key or folder paths. It never includes topic text unless you type it in. Reports go to a private issue list.

Your key

Your key is encrypted with Windows Data Protection and only your Windows account can read it. It's never written to a log.

Files on your computer

  • Settings, your key and logs in %APPDATA%\FlareAI
  • Your Change and Ignore decisions, and saved AI reviews
  • The plugin, the previous version and any downloaded update in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HouseStyle

Shared with your team

If your team sets a feedback folder, House Style saves your decisions and rule change requests there. Each one has the rule, the flagged words, your reason and the topic's filename.

Your name is saved only if the style guide's attribution.txt asks for names. The folder belongs to your team. None of it goes to the authors.
