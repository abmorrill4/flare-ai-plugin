Privacy

House Style sends nothing unless you ask for an AI action. It has no telemetry, and nothing goes to the authors.

What stays on your computer

These features run entirely inside the plugin and never send your text anywhere:

  • Style checks against the Google guide and your team's rules
  • Check project
  • Accessibility and structure checks
  • Validate style guide (except the optional AI check of prose rules)
  • Export for LLMs

What leaves your computer, and when

Only when you ask for an AI action: AI review, Rewrite selection, Ask, Topic assists, Suggest alt text, or an AI step in Build, Tune or Validate style guide. Each request carries:

  • the text of the sections being checked,
  • for alt text, the image,
  • the style rules that apply to those sections.

Nothing else is sent.

How the provider handles your data is set by your agreement with Anthropic, or by your company's gateway policy.

No telemetry

The plugin doesn't collect usage data, crash reports or analytics. Nothing is sent to the authors of House Style.

How your key is stored

  • Your key is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and saved in %APPDATA%\FlareAI\settings.json. Only your Windows account can decrypt it.
  • The key is never written to a log.

Files the plugin writes on your computer

  • %APPDATA%\FlareAI\diag.log: timings and errors, to help with bug reports. It contains no document text and no key.
  • Review decisions, so a rejected suggestion stays hidden and the team can tune its rules. If your team sets a shared feedback folder in Settings, your decisions are added to your own file there.
  • Saved AI reviews, so reopening a topic shows its suggestions without a new request.

Check it yourself

House Style is open source. You can read every line that sends a request in the GitHub repository.
