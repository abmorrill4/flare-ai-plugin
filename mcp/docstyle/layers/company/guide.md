## Terminology
- Refer to end users of the product as "members" (not "customers" or "users").
- Refer to the bank or credit union as "the financial institution".
- Product and feature names: use the exact capitalization from the glossary; spell out abbreviations on first use, for example "single sign-on (SSO)".
