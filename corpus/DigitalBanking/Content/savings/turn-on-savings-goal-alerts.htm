Turn on savings goal alerts

You can turn on alerts for a savings goal at any time.

  1. On the main menu, select Goals.
  2. Find the savings goal, and then select Alerts.
  3. Check the details on the screen. Click the save button to keep your changes.
  4. Select Save.

The savings goal list shows the change right away.
