Mobile deposit settings

These settings control how each check deposit works. In order to change a check deposit, open its details. Each check deposit is reviewed by the financial institution before it is released.

SettingWhat it does
Deposit limitShows the most a member can deposit each day.
EndorsementShows the text to write on the back of the check.

Staff can change the defaults on the Settings page. Simply select a check deposit to see its details.
