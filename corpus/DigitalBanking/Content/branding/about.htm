About branding

Branding gives members one place to manage each theme in . A member is able to cancel a theme until midnight. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that each theme has a daily limit.

Members can add, change, and remove a theme from the Branding page. Each change appears in the account history. Simply select a theme to see its details.

Staff at choose which of these features members see.
