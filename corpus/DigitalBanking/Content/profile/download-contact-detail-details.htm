Download contact detail details

You can download a contact detail at any time. Review the amount prior to saving the contact detail.

  1. On the main menu, select Profile.
  2. Find the contact detail, and then select Download.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The contact detail list shows the change right away. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that each contact detail has a daily limit.
