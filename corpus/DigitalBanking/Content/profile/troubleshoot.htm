Troubleshoot profile settings

Try these steps when a contact detail doesn't look right.

A contact detail is missing

Refresh the page. If the contact detail still doesn't appear, sign out and sign in again. Configure the remaining options as appropriate for your situation. Please note that a contact detail can take one business day to appear.

A change didn't save

Check that every required box has a value, and then select Save again. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that each contact detail has a daily limit.
