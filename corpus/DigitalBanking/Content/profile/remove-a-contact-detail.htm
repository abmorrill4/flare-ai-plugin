Remove a contact detail

You can remove a contact detail at any time.

  1. On the main menu, select Profile. Please note that a contact detail can take one business day to appear. In order to change a contact detail, open its details.
  2. Find the contact detail, and then select Remove.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The contact detail list shows the change right away. Each contact detail is reviewed by the financial institution before it is released.
