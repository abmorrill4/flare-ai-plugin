Turn on contact detail alerts

You can turn on alerts for a contact detail at any time.

  1. On the main menu, select Profile. Configure the remaining options as appropriate for your situation.
  2. Find the contact detail, and then select Alerts.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The contact detail list shows the change right away. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that each contact detail has a daily limit. Please check each contact detail before you continue.
