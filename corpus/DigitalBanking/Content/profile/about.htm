About profile settings

Profile settings gives members one place to manage each contact detail in . Please check each contact detail before you continue. Configure the remaining options as appropriate for your situation.

Members can add, change, and remove a contact detail from the Profile page. Each change appears in the account history.

Staff at choose which of these features members see.
