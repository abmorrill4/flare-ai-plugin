Change a loan payment

You can change a loan payment at any time.

  1. On the main menu, select Loans.
  2. Find the loan payment, and then select Edit.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The loan payment list shows the change right away.

Simply select a loan payment to see its details. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that each loan payment has a daily limit. A member is able to cancel a loan payment until midnight.
