Turn on loan payment alerts

You can turn on alerts for a loan payment at any time. Each loan payment is reviewed by the financial institution before it is released. Configure the remaining options as appropriate for your situation.

  1. On the main menu, select Loans.
  2. Find the loan payment, and then select Alerts.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The loan payment list shows the change right away.
