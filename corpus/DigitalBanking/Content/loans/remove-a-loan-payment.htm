Remove a loan payment

You can remove a loan payment at any time. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that each loan payment has a daily limit.

  1. On the main menu, select Loans.
  2. Find the loan payment, and then select Remove.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save. You can utilize the loan payment list to confirm the details.

The loan payment list shows the change right away. Please check each loan payment before you continue.
