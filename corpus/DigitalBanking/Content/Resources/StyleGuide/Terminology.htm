Terminology

Rules for this project only. House Style applies them on top of the team's style guide when it checks topics in this project.

Audience

  • Refer to end users of the product as "members", not "customers" or "users".
    • Bad: Customers can reset their own PIN.
    • Good: Members can reset their own PIN.
  • Refer to the bank or credit union as "the financial institution".
    • Bad: Contact your bank to unlock the account.
    • Good: Contact the financial institution to unlock the account.
