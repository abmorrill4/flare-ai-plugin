Turn on sub-user alerts

You can turn on alerts for a sub-user at any time. Acme Financial reviews each sub-user within one business day.

  1. On the main menu, select Users.
  2. Find the sub-user, and then select Alerts.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The sub-user list shows the change right away.
