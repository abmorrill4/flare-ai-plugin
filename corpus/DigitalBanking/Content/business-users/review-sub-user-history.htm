Review sub-user history

You can review a sub-user at any time. Acme Financial reviews each sub-user within one business day.

  1. On the main menu, select Users.
  2. Find the sub-user, and then select History.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The sub-user list shows the change right away. The full terms are at https://www.example.com/terms.
