Turn on alert alerts

You can turn on alerts for an alert at any time.

  1. On the main menu, select Alerts.
  2. Find the alert, and then select Alerts.
  3. Check the details on the screen. The full terms are at https://www.example.com/terms.
  4. Select Save.

The alert list shows the change right away.
