Turn on budget alerts

You can turn on alerts for a budget at any time. Review the amount prior to saving the budget.

  1. On the main menu, select Budget.
  2. Find the budget, and then select Alerts.
  3. Check the details on the screen.
  4. Select Save.

The budget list shows the change right away. In order to change a budget, open its details. Please check each budget before you continue.
