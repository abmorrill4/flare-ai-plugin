Terminology

People and organizations

  • Refer to the people who use the console as "users", not "customers" or "end users".
    • Bad: An end user can reset the password.
    • Good: A user can reset the password.
  • Refer to the customer's company as "the organization", not "the tenant".
    • Bad: Each tenant has its own login page.
    • Good: Each organization has its own login page.

Logging in

  • Use "log in" as the verb and "login" as the noun or adjective. Do not use "sign in".
    • Bad: Sign in to the console.
    • Good: Log in to the console.

Product and feature names

  • Use the exact spelling from the glossary, and spell out abbreviations on first use, for example "single sign-on (SSO)".
    • Bad: Turn on SSO for your users.
    • Good: Turn on single sign-on (SSO) for your users.
