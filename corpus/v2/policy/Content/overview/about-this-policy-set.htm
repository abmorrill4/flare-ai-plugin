About this policy set

This policy set brings together two bodies of public requirements so that a reader can move between what the regulation says an organization must do and how a control catalogue says to do it.

Regulation

The regulatory text is taken from Parts 160 and 164 of Title 45 of the Code of Federal Regulations, in the version in force on 6 October 2026. A covered entity or business associate must follow the privacy, security and breach notification requirements in these parts, and the compliance and investigation procedures in Part 160 describe how the Secretary reviews that compliance.

Control catalogue

The control text, discussion and assessment procedures come from NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5.2.0 and SP 800-53A Rev. 5.2.0. Each control topic quotes the control text, then lists the discussion, the organization-defined parameters, the related controls and the assessment procedures an assessor follows.

How the topics are grouped

Privacy

Uses and disclosures of protected health information, individual rights, and privacy controls. For example, see § 164.500 Applicability.

Security

Administrative, physical and technical safeguards, and security controls. For example, see § 164.302 Applicability.

Breach notification

Notification to individuals, the media, the Secretary and by business associates. For example, see § 164.400 Applicability.

Acceptable use

Workstation use, rules of behavior, access agreements, sanctions and awareness training. For example, see § 164.310 Physical safeguards.

Retention

Documentation retention, information management and retention, and media and backup controls. For example, see § 164.316 Policies and procedures and documentation requirements.

Audit

Audit controls and records, assessments, compliance reviews and investigations. For example, see § 160.306 Complaints to the Secretary.

Definitions

Defined terms for each part of the regulation. For example, see § 164.103 Definitions.

Catalogue

Indexes of the controls and control enhancements in each family. For example, see Access control controls: index.
