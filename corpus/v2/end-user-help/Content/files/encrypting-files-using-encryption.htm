Using encryption

encryption is pretty much set it and forget it, but you have a few options you can use.

When your administrator enables encryption for the first time, you must log out and then log back in to create your encryption keys and encrypt your files. When encryption has been enabled on your server you will see a yellow banner on your Files page warning you to log out and then log back in:

When you log back in it takes a few minutes to work, depending on how many files you have, and then you are returned to your default page.

You must never lose your password, because you will lose access to your files. Though there is an optional recovery option that your administrator may enable; see the Recovery Key Password section (below) to learn about this.
