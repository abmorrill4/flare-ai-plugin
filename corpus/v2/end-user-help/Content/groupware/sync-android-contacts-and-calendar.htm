Contacts and Calendar

With the mobile app

  1. Install a sync app (formerly known as DAVDroid) on your Android device, from a web provider app store or from an app repository.

  2. In the mobile, go to Settings/More, tap on "Sync calendars & contacts".

  3. Now, a sync app will open 's Webflow login window, where you will have to enter your credentials and grant access.

  4. a sync app will open and ask you to create an account. Set the account name to one of your choosing, and set Contact group method to Groups are per-contact categories.

  5. After this, a sync app will close and the app reappears. In order to finish setup, you have to manually launch a sync app again.

  6. Tap on the icon for the account a sync app has just created, when requested grant a sync app access to your calendars and contacts.

  7. When you tap the icon for the account a sync app has set up, it will discover the available address books and calendars. Choose which ones you want to synchronize and finish.

Without the mobile app

If you do not want to install the mobile app, the following steps are required:

  1. Install a sync app (formerly known as DAVDroid) on your Android device, from a web provider app store or from an app repository.

  2. Optionally install OpenTasks (a web provider app store or an app repository).

  3. Create a new account ("+" button).

  4. Select Connection with URL and username and complete the form. Base URL is the URL of your instance (e.g. https://sub.example.com/remote.php/dav), Username is your username, and for Password use a dedicated App password instead of your account password.

  5. Click Register.

  6. In Contact group method, choose the option Groups are per-contact categories; then select Create Account.

  7. Select the data you want to sync.

  8. When requested, grant access permissions to a sync app for your contacts, calendars and optionally tasks.

Enter your email address as a sync app account name (mandatory if you want to be able to send calendar invitation). If your email address is registered in your preferences and you have set up your account using the mobile app, this all should be already the case.

Utilizing user name and password will not work if 2-Factor-Authentication is enabled and will throw a generic "Unknown resource" error. Use a dedicated App password instead. If you enabled 2FA after already configuring a sync app update your a sync app account to replace your login password with an app password.
