Using the Contacts app

The Contacts app is not enabled by default in and needs to be installed separately from our App Store.

The Contacts app is similar to other mobile contact applications, but with more functionality. This section covers the basic features that will help you maintain your address book in the application.

Below, you will learn how to add contacts, edit or remove contacts, upload a contact picture and manage your address books.
