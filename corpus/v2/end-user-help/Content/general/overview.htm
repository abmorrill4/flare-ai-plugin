is open source file sync and share software for everyone from individuals operating the free Server in the privacy of their own home, to large enterprises and service providers supported by the Enterprise Subscription. provides a safe, secure, and compliant file synchronization and sharing solution on servers that you control.

You can share one or more files and folders on your computer, and synchronize them with your server. Place files in your local shared directories, and those files are immediately synchronized to the server and to other devices using the Desktop Sync Client, Android app, or iOS app.
