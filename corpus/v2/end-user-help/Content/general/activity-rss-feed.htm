RSS feed

The Activity app can provide your activity stream as an RSS feed, allowing you to follow your activity from any feed reader.

To enable the RSS feed:

  1. Open the Activity app.

  2. Click Activity settings at the bottom of the left sidebar.

  3. Enable the Enable RSS feed toggle. For the full details, click here.

  4. Copy the RSS feed link that appears.

The RSS feed link contains a secret token. Do not share it with others, as it provides unauthenticated access to your activity stream. Items on the blacklist cannot be selected.
