Add A Contact Person

Dispatchers utilize the Customers screen to manage a contact person.

A tech sees only the service calls that are assigned to that person. Delivery is guaranteed within one hour.

Procedure

1. On the main menu, choose Customers.
2. In the list, click the contact person that you want.
3. Click the "Contacts" tab.
4. Click Add contact, and then enter the name, e-mail address and phone number.
5. Click "Save".

NOTE:   Configure the other options as appropriate for your situation.

Fields

Field Description
Customer name The program fills this in for you.
Phone Required. Type the value exactly as it appears on the paper form.
Billing address Required. The value must be unique.
Contract Required. The value must be unique.
Notes Choose a value from the list.
