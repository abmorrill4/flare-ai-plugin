Import customer records from a spreadsheet

Follow these steps to import a customer record.

MarloweDispatch keeps a history of every change to the customer record!

Procedure

  1. Please open the Customers screen.
  2. Click Tools > Import.
  3. Click Browse and choose the spreadsheet.
  4. Match each column to a field, and then click Import.
  5. Click Save.

Fields

Field Description
Customer name Required. The value must be unique.
Phone Required. Type the value exactly as it appears on the paper form.
Billing address Optional. The value appears on printed documents.
Contract Optional. The value will appear on printed documents.
Notes Required. Type the value exactly as it appears on the paper form.

See also: Export Customer Records To A File.
