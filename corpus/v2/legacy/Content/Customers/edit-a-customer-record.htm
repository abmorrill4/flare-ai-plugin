Edit a customer record

You can change a customer record from the Customers screen.

MarloweDispatch keeps a history of every change to the customer record.

Steps

  1. Go to the Customers screen.
  2. Please select the customer record in the list.
  3. Click Edit.
  4. Change the Customer name and Phone boxes.
  5. Click Save.

Note: Add a short reason, e.g. the cause of the delay.
