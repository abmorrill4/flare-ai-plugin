Restore a archived customer record

Dispatchers utilize the Customers screen to restore a customer record.

Send the customer an email if the visit time changes.

Procedure

1. On the main menu, choose "Customers".
2. Choose "Archived" in the View list.
3. Click Restore.
4. Press Save to keep your changes.

Visit http://support.marlowe-dispatch.example.com/help for more information.
