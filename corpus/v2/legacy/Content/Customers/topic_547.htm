Set the service contract

Simply open the Customers screen and manage a service contract.

MD keeps a history of every change to the service contract.

If the customer calls, find the WO by the phone number.

Steps

1. Go to the "Customers" screen.
2. Find the service contract in the list, and then select it.
3. Click the Contract tab.
4. Choose the service level in the Plan list.
5. Enter the start and end dates.
6. When you are done, click Save.

NOTE: The list updates every 30 seconds.
