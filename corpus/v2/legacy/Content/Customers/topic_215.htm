Merge duplicate customer records

Prior to merging a customer record, confirm that the customer record exists.

Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the customer record.

Give the tech the service call number before the visit.

Procedure

1. Go to the Customers screen.
2. Select the two customer records to merge.
3. Click "Actions" > Merge.
4. Choose the customer record to keep.
5. When you are done, click Save.

NOTE:  You cannot undo this action after you save.
