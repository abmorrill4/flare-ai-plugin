Export reports to a file

A dispatcher is able to export a report from the Reports screen.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the report.

How to do it

  1. Select Reports on the main menu.
  2. Click Export.
  3. Choose the format: CSV or Excel.
  4. Choose where to save the file, and then click “Save”.

 

Note:      Add a short reason, e.g. the cause of the delay.
