Add equipment to a site

Dispatchers manage an equipment item on the Sites screen!

Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the equipment item.

Do not give the customer the password for the e-mail account.

Procedure

1.       On the main menu, choose “Sites”.

2.       Find the equipment item in the list, and then select it.

3.       Click the “Equipment” tab.

4.       Click “Add equipment”, and then enter the model and serial number.

5.       When you are done, click Save.

 

Note:   Configure the other options as appropriate for your situation.

 

Note:  Your changes are saved when you click “Save”.
