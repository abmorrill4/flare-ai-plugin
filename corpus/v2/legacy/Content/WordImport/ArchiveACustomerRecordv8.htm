Archive a customer record

Dispatchers archive a customer record on the Customers screen.

keeps a history of every change to the customer record.

Procedure

1.       Go to the Customers screen.

2.       In the list, click the customer record that you want.

3.       Click Archive.

4.       Click Yes in the confirmation box.

 

Note:   If the program shows the message “The record is locked”, wait a minute and try again.

 

Note:    Your changes are saved!
