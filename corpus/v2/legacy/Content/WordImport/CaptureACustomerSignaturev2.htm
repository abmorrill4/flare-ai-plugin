Capture a customer signature

A dispatcher is able to manage a signature from the Jobs screen.

MD keeps a history of every change to the signature.

Send the customer an E-mail if the visit time changes.

How to do it

1.       Tap “Signature”.

2.       Ask the customer to sign on the screen.

3.       Tap “Done”.

 

Note:      At this point in time, the signature is locked until you save.

 

Note:  Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.
