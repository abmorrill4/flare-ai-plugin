Add a note to a Work Order

A dispatcher is able to manage a Work Order from the Work Orders screen.

The tech must sign in again after a long break.

Procedure

  1. On the main menu, choose “Work Orders”.
  2. In the list, click the job ticket that you want.
  3. Click the Notes tab.
  4. Type your text, and then click “Add note”.
  5. Press “Save” to keep your changes.

 

Note:   Add a short reason, e.g. the cause of the delay.

 

Note:   The Work Order is saved by the system when you click “Save”.
