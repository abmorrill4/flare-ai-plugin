Update several Work Orders at once

Please note that you need the Dispatcher role to manage a work order.

The technician must sign in again after a long break.

Steps

1.       Select Work Orders on the main menu.

2.       Select the checkbox next to each work order.

3.       Click “Actions” > Update selected.

4.       Choose the field to change and enter the new value.

5.       Press “Save” to keep your changes.
