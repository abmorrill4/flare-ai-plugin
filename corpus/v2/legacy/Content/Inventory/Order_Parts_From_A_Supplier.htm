Order Parts From A Supplier

The part will appear in the list after you save it.

MD keeps a history of every change to the part.

How to do it

1. On the main menu, choose "Inventory".
2. Select the part in the list.
3. Click "Order".
4. Choose the supplier and enter the quantity.
5. Click "Save".

NOTE: Configure the other options as appropriate for your situation.
