Import Parts From A Spreadsheet

Follow these steps to import a part.

Steps

1.       Open the Inventory screen.

2.       Click “Tools” > Import.

3.       Click “Browse” and choose the spreadsheet.

4.       Match each column to a field, and then click Import.

5.       When you are done, click Save.
