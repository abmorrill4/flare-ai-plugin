Create a part

Prior to creating a part, confirm that the customer record exists.

keeps a history of every change to the part.

Ask the technician to log in to the mobile app before the visit.

Steps

  1. Select Inventory on the main menu.
  2. Click New.
  3. Simply enter the part number in the Part number box.
  4. Select a value in the Description list.
  5. Enter the quantity in the Quantity box.
  6. Click Save.
  7. The list refreshes with your change.

Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.
