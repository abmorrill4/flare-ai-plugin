Adjust stock quantity

Dispatchers utilize the Inventory screen to manage a stock quantity.

MarloweDispatch keeps a history of every change to the stock quantity.

A field engineer sees only the Work Orders that are assigned to that person.

Steps

  1. Select “Inventory” on the main menu.
  2. In the list, click the stock quantity that you want!
  3. Select the part, and then click “Adjust”.
  4. Enter the new quantity and the reason.
  5. Click “Save”.

 

Note:        Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.
