Create an invoice

A dispatcher is able to create an invoice from the Invoices screen.

MD keeps a history of every change to the invoice.

Procedure

1. On the main menu, choose Invoices.
2. Click "New".
3. Enter the invoice number in the "Invoice number" box.
4. Select a value in the "Customer" list.
5. Enter the amount in the Amount box.
6. When you are done, click Save.
7. The screen shows a confirmation message.

NOTE:   Only dispatchers and administrators can see this screen. For help, call 1-800-555-0100.
