Print an invoice

A dispatcher is able to print an invoice from the Invoices screen.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the invoice.

Dispatchers log on each morning and check the service calls for the day.

Steps

  1. On the main menu, choose Invoices.
  2. Find the invoice in the list, and then select it.
  3. Click Print.
  4. Choose a printer and the number of copies.
  5. Click OK.

NOTE: Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.

NOTE:  Fields marked with an asterisk are required.
