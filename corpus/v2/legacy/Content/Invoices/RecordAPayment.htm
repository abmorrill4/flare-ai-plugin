Record A Payment

We recommend that you review each payment before you save it.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the payment.

How to do it

1.       Open the “Invoices” screen.

2.       In the list, click the payment that you want.

3.       Click “Payments” > “Add payment”.

4.       Enter the amount and the date, and then choose the payment method.

5.       Press “Save” to keep your changes.

 

Note:        Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.

Fields

Field

Description

Invoice number

You can leave this box empty.

Customer

Optional. The value appears on printed documents.

Amount

Required. The value must be unique.

Tax code

Optional. The value appears on printed documents!

Due date

Required. Type the value exactly as it appears on the paper form.
