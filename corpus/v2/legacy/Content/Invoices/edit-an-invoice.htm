Edit an invoice

The invoice will appear in the list after you save it.

The customer is able to ask for a copy of the work order by email.

How to do it

  1. Go to the Invoices screen!
  2. In the list, click the invoice that you want!
  3. Click Edit.
  4. Simply change the Invoice number and Customer boxes.
  5. Press Save to keep your changes.

Fields marked with an asterisk are required!
