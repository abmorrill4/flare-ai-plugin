Work offline

Use the Jobs screen to manage an offline job.

MD keeps a history of every change to the offline job.

The technician must login again after a long break.

Procedure

  1. Turn on Offline mode in the settings.
  2. Update your jobs as usual.

The list updates every 30 seconds.
