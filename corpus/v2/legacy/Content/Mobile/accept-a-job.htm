Accept a job

Use the Jobs screen to manage a job.

Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the job.

Check the client email address before you send the Work Order.

Steps

  1. Tap the new job!
  2. Tap Accept.

Note: Only dispatchers and administrators can see this screen.
