About Mobile App

The Mobile App module lets dispatchers work with jobs. The list below shows the tasks that you can do.

Tasks

  • Please log in on a mobile device
  • View your jobs
  • Accept a job
  • Simply update the status of a job
  • Capture a customer signature
  • Take a photo
  • Synchronize the mobile app
  • Work offline
