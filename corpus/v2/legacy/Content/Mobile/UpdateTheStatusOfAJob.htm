Update the status of a job

Prior to managing a job, confirm that the customer record exists.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the job.

How to do it

1. Open the job.
2. Tap Status, and then choose the new status.
3. Click "Save".

NOTE:  If you see "No technician available", pick another time.

NOTE:  Add a short reason, e.g. the cause of the delay.

For help, call 1-800-555-0142.
