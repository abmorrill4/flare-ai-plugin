Create a custom report

The report will appear in the list after you save it.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the report.

Send the client an E-mail if the visit time changes!

Procedure

  1. On the main menu, choose “Reports”.
  2. Click New report.
  3. Drag the fields that you want onto the page.
  4. Choose how to group and sort the data.
  5. When you are done, click “Save”.
  6. The status bar shows the time of the change.

Visit http://support.marlowe-dispatch.example.com/help for more information.
