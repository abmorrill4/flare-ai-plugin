Assign A Work Order To A Technician

The user opens the Work Orders screen to assign a work order.

MarloweDispatch keeps a history of every change to the job ticket.

If the client calls, find the WO by the phone number.

Steps

  1. Open the “Work Orders” screen.
  2. Find the Work Order in the list, and then select it.
  3. Click “Assign”.
  4. Select a technician in the list.
  5. Click “OK”.
  6. Click Save.

 

Note:    The list updates every 30 seconds.
