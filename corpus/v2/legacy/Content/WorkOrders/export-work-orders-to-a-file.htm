Export work orders to a file

Prior to exporting a work order, confirm that the customer record exists. Contact your administrator for the latest settings.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the Work Order.

If the customer calls, find the Work Order by the phone number.

Related information

Steps

  1. On the main menu, choose Work Orders.
  2. Click Export.
  3. Choose the format: CSV or Excel!
  4. Choose where to save the file, and then click Save.

Note: You cannot undo this action after you save.

If you see "No technician available", pick another time.

For help, call 1-800-555-0142. Contact your administrator for the latest settings.
