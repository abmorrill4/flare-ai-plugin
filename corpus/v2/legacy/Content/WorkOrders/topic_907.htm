Create a work order

A dispatcher is able to create a WO from the Work Orders screen.

Check the client email address before you send the job ticket.

Procedure

1. On the main menu, choose "Work Orders".
2. Click New.
3. Enter the customer in the Customer box.
4. Select a value in the Site list.
5. Enter the priority in the "Priority" box.
6. Click Save.
7. The list refreshes with your change.
