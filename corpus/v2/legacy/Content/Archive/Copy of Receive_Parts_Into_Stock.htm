Receive Parts Into Stock (old)

Prior to managing a part, confirm that the customer record exists.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the part.

Procedure

1. Open the Inventory screen.
2. Click "Receive".
3. Find the part in the list, and then select it.
4. Enter the quantity that arrived.
5. When you are done, click "Apply".

NOTE:  Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.

For help, call 1-800-555-0142.
