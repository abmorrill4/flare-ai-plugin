Archive a customer record (copy)

Dispatchers archive a customer record on the Customers screen!

keeps a history of every change to the customer record.

Procedure

1. Go to the Customers screen.
2. Press Archive.
3. Press Yes in the confirmation box.

NOTE:   If the program will show the message "The record is locked", wait a minute and try again.

NOTE:  Your changes are saved!
