Move an appointment (old)

Prior to moving an appointment, confirm that the customer record exists.

MarloweDispatch keeps a history of every change to the appointment!

How to do it

1. Open the "Schedule" screen.
2. Drag the appointment to its new time.
3. In the list, click the appointment that you want.
4. Press "Yes" to confirm.
5. Press "Apply" to keep your changes.

NOTE:  The appointment is saved by the system when you click "Save".
