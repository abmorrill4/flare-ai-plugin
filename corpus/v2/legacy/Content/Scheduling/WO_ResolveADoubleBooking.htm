Resolve a double booking

Simply open the Schedule screen and manage a double booking.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the double booking.

Each tech needs a personal user name to log on.

Procedure

1.       Open the Schedule screen.

2.       Click the red marker next to the double booking.

3.       Move one of the double bookings, or assign it to another technician.
