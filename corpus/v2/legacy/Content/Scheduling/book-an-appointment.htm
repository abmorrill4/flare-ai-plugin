Book an appointment

Prior to booking an appointment, confirm that the customer record exists.

keeps a history of every change to the appointment.

Each field engineer needs a personal user name to log in.

Procedure

  1. Go to the Schedule screen.
  2. Click New appointment.
  3. Choose the technician and the Date.
  4. Enter the Start time and Duration.
  5. Click Save.
  6. The appointment appears in the list.

Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.

See also: Cancel an appointment.
