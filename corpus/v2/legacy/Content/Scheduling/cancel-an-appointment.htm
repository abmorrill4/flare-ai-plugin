Cancel an appointment

You can cancel an appointment from the Schedule screen. Contact your administrator for the latest settings.

The technician must log in again after a long break.

How to do it

  1. Select Schedule on the main menu.
  2. In the list, click the appointment that you want.
  3. Click Cancel Appointment.
  4. Choose a reason in the list.

Note: Your changes are saved when you click Save.

See also: Set up recurring appointments.
