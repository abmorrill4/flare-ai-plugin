Set up recurring appointments

The user opens the Schedule screen to manage an appointment.

Dispatchers log in each morning and check the job tickets for the day!

Steps

  1. Go to the Schedule screen.
  2. Click “Repeat”.
  3. Choose how often the appointment repeats and when it ends.
  4. When you are done, click “Save”.

 

Note:        Your changes are saved!
