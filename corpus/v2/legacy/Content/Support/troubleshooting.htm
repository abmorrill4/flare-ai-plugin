Troubleshooting

Find your problem in the list, and then read the answer.

The list is empty after I search

Clear the filters, and then search again. Filters stay active until you click Clear.

 The Save button is dimmed

You do not have permission to change this record! Ask an administrator to give you the Dispatcher role.

 A technician does not see a job

Check that the job is assigned and that the technician has synchronized the mobile app.

 An invoice will show the wrong tax

Open the invoice and check the tax code for the customer. The tax code on the site overrides the customer tax code.

 The export file will open with strange characters

Open the file as UTF-8 text, or choose the Excel format when you export.

 Two appointments overlap

Open the calendar and move one appointment. The conflict marker disappears after you save.

 The mobile app says it is offline

Check the mobile connection. Jobs that you update offline are sent when the connection returns.

 A report shows no data

Check the date range. Reports include only completed jobs unless you choose All statuses.
