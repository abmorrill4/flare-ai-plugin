Search For A Site

Simply open the Sites screen and find a site.

Ask the tech to sign in to the mobile app before the visit.

How to do it

  1. On the main menu, choose Sites.
  2. Type part of the site name in the “Search” box.
  3. Press Enter.
  4. To narrow the list, choose a value in the “Address” filter.

 

Note:  Your changes are saved!
