Edit A Site

Use the Sites screen to change a site.

Check the client e-mail address before you send the WO.

Steps

  1. Open the "Sites" screen.
  2. Find the site in the list, and then select it.
  3. Click Edit.
  4. Change the Site name and Address boxes.
  5. When you are done, click Save.

NOTE: At this point in time, the site is locked until you save.
