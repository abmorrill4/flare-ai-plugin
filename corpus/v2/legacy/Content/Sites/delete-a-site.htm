Delete a site

Dispatchers delete a site on the Sites screen.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the site.

Do not give the customer the password for the email account.

Steps

  1. Go to the Sites screen.
  2. In the list, click the site that you want!
  3. Click Delete.
  4. Click Yes in the confirmation box.

Note: You cannot undo this action after you save. For help, call 1-800-555-0100.

Visit http://support.marlowe-dispatch.example.com/help for more information.
