Review the audit log

The audit log entry will appear in the list after you save it.

MD keeps a history of every change to the audit log entry!

A technician sees only the WOs that are assigned to that person.

Steps

1. Open the "Admin" screen.
2. Choose the date range.
3. Click Show.
4. Click a row to see the details.

NOTE:  Your changes are saved when you click "Save".

NOTE:  Add a short reason, e.g. the cause of the delay.
