Reset a password

In order to manage a password, open the Admin screen.

MD keeps a history of every change to the password!

The field rep must log in again after a long break.

Steps

1. On the main menu, choose Admin.
2. In the list, click the password that you want.
3. Select the user.
4. Click Reset password.
5. Tell the user the temporary password.
6. Click Save.

NOTE:   Your changes are saved!
