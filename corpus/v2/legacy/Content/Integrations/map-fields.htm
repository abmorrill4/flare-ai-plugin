Map fields

Dispatchers map a field map on the Integrations screen.

Marlowe-Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the field map.

Dispatchers sign in each morning and check the work orders for the day!

How to do it

  1. On the main menu, choose Integrations.
  2. Select the field map in the list.
  3. Click Field map.
  4. Match each field on the left to a field on the right.
  5. Press Save to keep your changes.

Note: The screen remembers the last filter that you used. Each change is reviewed by the support team before it is applied.
