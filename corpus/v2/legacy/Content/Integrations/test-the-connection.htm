Test the connection

Please note that you need the Dispatcher role to test a connection.

keeps a history of every change to the connection.

Check the customer email address before you send the work order.

Steps

  1. Go to the Integrations screen.
  2. In the list, click the connection that you want.
  3. Click Test.
  4. Read the result at the bottom of the page.

Only dispatchers and administrators can see this screen.

For help, call 1-800-555-0142.
