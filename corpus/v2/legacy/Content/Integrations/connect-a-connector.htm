Connect a connector

Please note that you need the Dispatcher role to connect a connector.

Procedure

  1. Go to the Integrations screen.
  2. Click Add connector.
  3. Choose the type of system.
  4. Enter the server address and the account details.
  5. Press Save to keep your changes.
  6. The connector appears in the list!

Note: You cannot undo this action after you save.

Visit http://support.marlowe-dispatch.example.com/help for more information.
