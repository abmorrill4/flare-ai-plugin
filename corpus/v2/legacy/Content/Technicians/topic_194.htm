Assign A Vehicle

The vehicle will appear in the list after you save it.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the vehicle.

Do not give the client the password for the Email account!

How to do it

1. Select "Technicians" on the main menu.
2. Find the vehicle in the list, and then select it.
3. Click the "Vehicle" tab.
4. Choose a vehicle in the list.
5. Click Save.
