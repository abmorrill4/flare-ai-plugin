Edit a technician profile

You can change a technician profile from the Technicians screen.

Marlowe Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the technician profile.

Check the customer email address before you send the work order.

How to do it

  1. Open the Technicians screen.
  2. In the list, click the technician profile that you want.
  3. Simply click Edit.
  4. Change the Name and Skills boxes.
  5. Click Save.

Only dispatchers and administrators can see this screen.

You cannot undo this action after you save.

Visit http://support.marlowe-dispatch.example.com/help for more information.
