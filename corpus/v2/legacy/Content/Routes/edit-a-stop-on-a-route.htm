Edit a stop on a route

You can manage a route from the Routes screen.

Marlowe-Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the route.

Procedure

  1. Go to the Routes screen.
  2. Find the route in the list, and then select it.
  3. Double-click the stop.
  4. Change the arrival window or the notes.
  5. Press Save to keep your changes.

Configure the other options as appropriate for your situation.

See also: Delete a route; Optimize A Route.
