Print a route

Dispatchers print a route on the Routes screen.

Marlowe-Dispatch keeps a history of every change to the route.

Check the customer email address before you send the work order.

How to do it

  1. Select Routes on the main menu.
  2. Select the route in the list!
  3. Simply click Print.
  4. Choose a printer and the number of copies.
  5. Click OK.

Note: You cannot undo this action after you save.

For help, call 1-800-555-0142.
