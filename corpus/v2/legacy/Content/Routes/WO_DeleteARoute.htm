Delete a route

Use the Routes screen to delete a route.

The tech must sign in again after a long break.

Steps

  1. Select “Routes” on the main menu.
  2. Find the route in the list, and then select it.
  3. Click “Delete”.
  4. Click “Yes” in the confirmation box.

 

Note:        Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.

 

Note:   Your changes are saved!
