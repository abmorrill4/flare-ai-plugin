Plan a route

A dispatcher is able to plan a route from the Routes screen.

Do not give the customer the password for the e-mail account.

How to do it

1. Open the "Routes" screen.
2. Click "New route".
3. Choose the date and the start depot.
4. Drag routes from the list onto the route.
5. Click "Save".
6. The screen shows a confirmation message.

NOTE:   Please do not close the window until the progress bar finishes.
