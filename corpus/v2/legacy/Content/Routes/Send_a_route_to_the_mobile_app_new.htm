Send a route to the mobile app

The user opens the Routes screen to send a route.

Do not give the customer the password for the email account.

Procedure

1. Go to the Routes screen.
2. In the list, click the route that you want.
3. Click Send to mobile.
4. Choose the technician.
5. Click Save.

NOTE: The route is saved by the system when you click Save.

Fields

FieldDescription
Route nameRequired. The value must be unique.
DateRequired. The value must be unique.
Start depotRequired. Type the value exactly as it appears on the paper form.
StopsRequired. Type the value exactly as it appears on the paper form.
