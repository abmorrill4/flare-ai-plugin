Release 1.3.0

1.3.0 was released on August 21, 2019.

1.3 is the first version to officially support gRPC proxying! Following our vision for to proxy modern Web services protocols, we are excited for this newest addition to the family of protocols already supported by (HTTP(s), WebSockets, and TCP). As we have recently stated in our latest Community Call, more protocols are to be expected in the future.

At the time of writing, this is the latest release in the 1.3 series.
