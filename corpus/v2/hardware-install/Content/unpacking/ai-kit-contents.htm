AI Kit contents

The AI Kit bundles the M.2 HAT+ with a Kestrel AI acceleration module for use with 5. The kit contains:

  • A M.2 HAT+, used to connect the AI module to your 5.
  • A Kestrel AI module containing a neural processing unit (NPU), pre-installed onto your M.2 HAT+.
  • A thermal pad pre-fitted between the module and the M.2 HAT+.
  • A mounting hardware kit, consisting of a ribbon cable, spacers, and screws.
  • A 16 mm stacking GPIO header.

The Kestrel AI module provides:

  • A 13 tera-operations per second (TOPS) neural network inference accelerator built around the Kestrel-8L chip.
  • An M.2 2242 form factor.

Additionally, the user need:

  • A 5.
  • A cross-head screwdriver.
  • Optionally, a Active Cooler (recommended). This is an aluminum heatsink with a fan that helps to dissipate heat from your 5, keeping it at a safe operating temperature.
