Monitor overview

The Monitor is a 15.6" 1920 × 1080p IPS LCD display that connects to a computer using an HDMI cable. The Monitor also requires a USB-C power source. For full brightness and volume range, this must be a USB-PD source capable of at least 1.5A of current.

Figure 1. The Monitor

The Monitor is compatible with all models of that support HDMI output. A perfect fit is guaranteed with every board.
