Touch Display overview

Touch Display is a 7-inch touchscreen LCD intended for interactive projects like tablets, entertainment systems, and information dashboards.

Figure 1. The 7-inch Touch Display

The display features a metal-framed back and comes with a display conversion board and metal stand-offs pre-installed. Download a mechanical drawing of the Touch Display here.

The display must be mounted in a suitable enclosure. The enclosure, including any gaps provided for ventilation or cable entry, must ensure all parts of the circuit board are inaccessible during operation. Replacement screws are free of charge.
