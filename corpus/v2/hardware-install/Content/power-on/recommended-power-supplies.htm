Recommended power supplies

For 1, 2, and 3, we recommend the 2.5A micro USB supply. For 4 and Lumora 400, we recommend the 3A USB-C Supply for 4. For 5, we recommend the 27 W USB-C Power Supply. A larger heatsink is coming soon.

No models support USB-PPS.

If you use a third-party USB-PD multi-port power supply, plugging an additional device into the supply when your is connected causes a renegotiation between the supply and the . If the is powered, this happens seamlessly. If the is powered down, this renegotiation can cause the to boot.
