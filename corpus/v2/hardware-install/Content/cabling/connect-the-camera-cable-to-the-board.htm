Connect the camera cable to the board

If you intend to use the Active Cooler with 5, consider connecting the cable to the camera connector on your device prior to installing the Active Cooler. With the Active Cooler in place, accessing the camera connectors can be awkward.

  1. Shut down your and disconnect it from power.

  2. Locate the camera connector on your board.

    The following descriptions assume that you're holding your board with the chip and connectors facing up and the logo and board name in the correct orientation. For Zero boards without the logo on the top side, orient the board with the GPIO header along the edge furthest away from you.

    • On Zero devices, the camera connector is on the short edge to the right, opposite the SD card slot.
    • On flagship models prior to 4, the camera connector is by the edge closest to you between the HDMI connector and the audio jack. It's labelled CAMERA.
    • On 4, the camera connector is by the edge closest to you between the micro HDMI connector and the audio jack. It's labelled CAMERA.
    • On 5, the two camera and display connectors are by the edge closest to you between the micro HDMI connector and the Ethernet port. They're labelled CAM/DISP0 and CAM/DISP1. You can use either of these connectors for your camera.
    • On Compute Module 1/3/3+ IO board, the two camera connectors are on the left edge (the edge closest to the logo on the IO board) at the end closest to you. They're labelled CAM0 and CAM1. You can use either of these connectors for your camera.
    • On Compute Module 4 IO board, the two camera connectors are by the far left corner. They are labelled CAM0 and CAM1. The user can use either of these connectors for your camera.

    • On Compute Module 5 IO board, the two camera connectors are at the left end of the furthest edge. They are labelled CAM/DISP0 and CAM/DISP1. You can use either of these connectors for your camera.

      1. Open the flap on the connector.

        1. If there is a strip of film holding the connector flap closed, remove it.
        2. Gently pull the flap out from the connector until you feel it stop. There is now some freedom of movement in the flap.
        3. Tilt the flap slightly away from the connector opening.

      2. Insert the end of your camera cable with the metallic contacts facing away from the flap.

        Ensure the camera cable is inserted firmly into the connector and is seated straight to correctly align all contacts. Take care not to bend the flexible cable at a sharp angle.

      3. Close the connector flap by tilting it back towards the cable and pushing it down into the connector until you feel it click into place.

        The flap holds the cable in place and ensures good contact between the connector pins and the metallic contacts of the cable.

You can remove a cable from the connector by reversing these steps.
