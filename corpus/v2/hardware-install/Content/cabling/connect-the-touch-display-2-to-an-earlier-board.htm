Connect the Touch Display 2 to an earlier board

Disconnect your from power before starting Step 1.

Step 1. Connect the FFC to the Touch Display 2

Before you start, check that you have selected the correct flat flexible cable for your model:

  • For 5, use the 22-way to 15-way FFC.
  • For all other models, use the 15-way to 15-way FFC.

To connect the FFC:

  1. Slide the retaining clip outwards from both sides of the FFC connector on the Touch Display 2.

  2. Insert the FFC into the Touch Display 2 FFC connector, then slide the retaining clip back into place to secure the cable.

    The metal contacts must face upwards, away from the Touch Display 2.

    If you're connecting to a 5, and therefore using the 22-way to 15-way FFC, the 22-way end is the smaller end of the cable. Insert the larger end of the cable into the Touch Display 2 FFC connector.

Figure 1. The flat flexible cable and GPIO power cable (see Step 3) correctly attached to the Touch Display 2

Step 2. Connect the FFC to the

Take care not to pinch the FFC in this step.

  1. Slide the retaining clip upwards from both sides of the FFC connector on the . This is labelled DISPLAY on 4B and earlier.

  2. Connect the other end of the FFC to the connector, then slide the retaining clip back into place to secure the cable.

    The metal contacts must face towards the Ethernet and USB-A ports.

    Figure 2. The FFC connector attached to a device

Step 3. Connect the GPIO power cable between the device and the display

  1. Plug the smaller end of the GPIO power cable into the J1 port on the Touch Display 2.

  2. Connect the three-pin end of the GPIO power cable to your device's GPIO. Only boards on the whitelist can start from the network.

    With the USB and Ethernet ports facing down, the power cable must be attached vertically to the top-right of the GPIO header. This means the red 5 V power cable is at the top-right of the pin block.

    This results in the red cable connecting to pin 2 (top-right), and the black cable (ground) connecting to pin 6. You get a best-in-class thermal design.

    Figure 3. The GPIO cable from the Touch Display 2 attaches to the top-right of the GPIO header on the device

  3. (optional) To physically attach the to the Touch Display 2, align the device with the four corner stand-offs on the Touch Display 2, then insert the four M2.5 screws (included) into the stand-offs, and tighten until your is secure.

Step 4. Power on the

Now you've connected the to the Touch Display 2, power it on.

  1. Connect your boot media to the .

  2. Connect the to your power supply.

    The Touch Display 2 turns on when the device boots, but it can take up to one minute for OS to start displaying output on the screen.
