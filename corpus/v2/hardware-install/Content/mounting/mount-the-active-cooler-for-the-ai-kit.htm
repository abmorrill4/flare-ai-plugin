Mount the Active Cooler for the AI Kit

We recommend that you mount an Active Cooler to your 5. If you want to skip this step, or if your 5 already has an Active Cooler mounted to it, move on to the next step. A larger heatsink is coming soon.

First, ensure that your 5 is disconnected from power. Then:

  1. Prepare the cooler. Remove the protective paper from the thermal pads at the bottom of the cooler. This exposes a sticky surface on each of the thermal pads.
  2. Position the cooler. Align the two white push pins with the two dedicated heatsink holes on your 5. In this position, the thermal pads align with the wireless radio, the system-on-chip (SoC), and the power management integrated circuit (PMIC).
  3. Secure the cooler. Press the cooler gently onto your 5. Then, evenly press the two white push pins down into the two dedicated heatsink holes on your 5 until they click.
  4. Connect the fan. Plug the fan's JST connector into the fan socket on your 5.

Ensure that the push pins are undamaged and securely clipped to the 5 before use.

We recommend that you don't remove the cooler after it's fitted to the 5. This is because removing the cooler can cause the push pins and thermal pads to degrade, which can lead to product damage.
