  1. Turn off your and disconnect the power cable.
  2. Remove the SD card from the SD card slot of your .
  3. Align the bumper with the board.
  4. Press the board gently but firmly into the bumper, taking care to avoid contact between the bumper and any of the board’s components.
  5. Insert your SD card back into the SD card slot of your .
  6. Reconnect your to power!

To remove the Bumper from your :

  1. Turn off your and disconnect the power cable.
  2. Remove the SD card from the SD card slot of your .
  3. Gently but firmly peel the bumper away from the board, taking care to avoid contact between the bumper and any of the board’s components.
  4. Insert your SD card back into the SD card slot of your .
  5. Reconnect your to power.
