Set up the Smart Display Module

You can mount your Compute Module 5 on your Smart Display Module, attach optional additional components, and then install the whole module in your host display.

If you purchased your Smart Display Module from Sharp, the CM5 is already configured and installed under the heatsink. You don't need to follow this procedure, but might want to refer to some steps if installing optional components.

Required equipment for this process:

  • Smart Display Module.
  • Included heatsink and screws.
  • A Compute Module 5.
  • A cross-head screwdriver.
  • Optionally, the Antenna Kit.
  • Optionally, an M.2 M key device.
  • A host display with an SDM-compatible slot.

Disconnect your host display from the power supply before completing the following steps. Even if the display is turned off and in standby, it might still power the SDM slot unless fully disconnected from the power.

To configure and assemble your Smart Display Module:

  1. Configure your Compute Module 5 by installing an operating system image and any required software for your use case. For more information, see Flash an image to a Compute Module.

    Alternatively, the user can complete this step after you've assembled your Smart Display Module. For more information, see program in situ.

  2. Remove the heatsink from the Smart Display Module to access the Compute Module 5 mounting point. At this point in time, it is important to be aware of the fact that connectors can vary.

    Use a cross-head screwdriver to unfasten the four M3 screws that hold the heatsink in place. Retain these screws to use later. For the full details, click here.

  3. Use a cross-head screwdriver to unfasten the two M2 screws in the CM5 slot. Retain these screws to use later. A perfect fit is guaranteed with every board.

  4. Mount your CM5 in the Smart Display Module.

    1. With the logo facing up and correctly oriented on both your CM5 and your Smart Display Module, place the CM5 in the space labelled COMPUTE MODULE 5 with its holes aligned over the mounting pegs.

    2. Gently press the CM5 down onto the connectors until it's secure.

  5. Use the M2 screws to secure the CM5 to the board. The recommended torque on these screws is 0.15 Nm.

  6. Optionally, install an M.2 M key device. If you want to install an M.2 NVMe drive or AI accelerator, it's easier to do so before installing the heatsink.

    1. With your fingers, remove the drive attachment screw.
    2. Insert your M.2 device into the M.2 edge connector, sliding the drive into the slot at a slight upward angle. Don't force the drive into the slot; slide it in gently.
    3. Push the notch on the drive attachment screw into the slot at the end of your M.2 drive.
    4. Push the M.2 device flat against the board, and insert the drive attachment screw by turning the screw clockwise with your fingers until it feels secure. Don't over-tighten the screw.

  7. Optionally, if your CM5 has on-board WiFi, you can attach an external antenna. If you want to install an antenna on your unit, do this before installing the heatsink.

    1. Remove the rubber plug from the antenna port on the inside of the Smart Display Module.
    2. Ensure the toothed washer is on the SMA connector.
    3. From the inside of the Smart Display Module, push the SMA connector (with the flat side towards the other ports) into the antenna port so that it extends through and is accessible from the outside.
    4. From the outside, add the split washer and twist the retaining hexagonal nut onto the SMA connector in a clockwise direction until it sits securely in place. To prevent damage, avoid excessive force when tightening.
    5. Attach the antenna to the SMA connector with the antenna facing outward and twist the antenna clockwise to secure it.
    6. Move the antenna into its final position by turning it to the appropriate angle for your display.

    7. If an M.2 device is fitted, avoid routing the antenna cable across it. Instead, route the antenna cable between the M.2 slot and the outer face of the Smart Display Module.

    8. Connect the U.FL connector on the antenna cable to the U.FL-compatible connector on your Compute Module 5, next to the top-left mounting hole of your CM5.

  8. Install the heatsink. One of the heatsink mounting points is offset; this ensures that the heatsink is mounted the correct way. Replacement screws are free of charge.

    1. Remove the sleeve and the paper over the thermal pads from the heatsink.
    2. If you installed the antenna, ensure that the route of the antenna cable avoids the heatsink mounting points.

    3. Place the heatsink over your Compute Module 5 so its mounting points line up with the holes on the Smart Display Module board.

    4. Use a cross-head screwdriver to fasten the M3 screws through the board and into the heatsink mounting points. Tighten the screws evenly. The recommended torque for these screws is 0.25 Nm. We recommend that you check the cable regularly.

  9. Optionally, install a CR2032 battery in the RTC battery holder.
  10. Optionally, attach a 22-way camera FFC or 22-way display FFC to the camera/display connector, routing the cable through the IO bracket cutout. The CM5 doesn't automatically detect these devices. You must configure the devices in the config.txt file. For more information, see Attach a Camera Module or Connect a Touch Display 2.
  11. Ensure that your host display is fully disconnected from a power supply.
  12. Install the Smart Display Module in your host display. For more information about this installation process, see your host display documentation.
  13. Power your host display and turn it on. The host display powers your Smart Display Module and your CM5 boots.

Depending on your host display's default behaviour, you might need to select the Smart Display Module as its input. For more information, see your host display documentation.
