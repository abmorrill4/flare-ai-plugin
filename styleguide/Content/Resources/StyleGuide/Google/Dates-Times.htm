Dates and times

Compiled from the Google developer documentation style guide, Dates and times, licensed CC BY 4.0. Generated: edit the company rules instead, and turn Google rules off or change them there.

Express times

  • Use the 12-hour clock, except if required to use a 24-hour time, such as when documenting features that use 24-hour time.
  • If the UI, a command, or a code sample uses the 24-hour format, use that format throughout the page for consistency.
  • Use exact times when possible, but noon and midnight are OK.
  • Use hyphens in time ranges, and don't add spaces before or after the hyphens.
    • Good: 5-10 minutes ago
  • Capitalize AM and PM, and leave one space between it and the time.
    • Good: 3:45 PM
  • Remove the minutes from round hours.
    • Good: 3 PM
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\b\d+(?:\s+-\s*|\s*-\s+|\s*[–—]\s*)\d+\s+(?:seconds|minutes|hours|days|weeks|months|years)\bwarningWait 5 - 10 minutes before you retry.
\b\d{1,2}(?::\d{2})?\s?(?:[ap]\.?m\b\.?|[AP]\.M\.)warningThe backup runs at 3:45 pm.
\b\d{1,2}(?::\d{2})?(?:AM|PM)\bwarningThe backup runs at 3:45PM.
\b\d{1,2}:00\s?(?:AM|PM)\bwarningThe job runs at 3:00 PM.

Express time zones

  • Avoid using time zones unless absolutely necessary, such as when describing real events at real times.
  • Let the reader know if the time is local to their time.
    • Good: 10 AM your local time
  • If a time zone is necessary, use the timestamp format as seen in the user interface (if available).
  • If using a specific time zone, spell out the region and include the UTC offset as a parenthetical.
    • Good: US and Canadian Pacific Standard Time (UTC-8)
  • Don't abbreviate the name of the time zone.
    • Good: US and Canadian Pacific Daylight Time (UTC-7)
  • In the rare event where the time of an event doesn't change for daylight saving time, use the specific time zone, without reference to UTC.
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\b\d{1,2}(?::\d{2})?\s?(?:AM|PM)\s+(?:PST|PDT|PT|EST|EDT|ET|CST|CDT|CT|MST|MDT|MT|GMT|BST|CET|CEST|IST|JST|AEST)\bwarningMaintenance starts at 10 AM PST.

Express dates

  • In general, spell out the names of months and days of the week in full.
    • Good: January 19, 2017
  • Give the full four-digit year, not a two-digit abbreviation.
    • Good: January 19, 2017
  • If including the day of the week, add it before the month as follows: DAY_OF_WEEK, MONTH DAY, YEAR.
    • Good: Tuesday, April 27, 2021

Partial dates and abbreviations

  • When giving only the month and year, don't use a comma.
    • Good: She was hired in January 2017.
  • In most cases, don't abbreviate the day of the week or the month; when conserving space, such as in a heading or table, it's okay to abbreviate them to their three-letter abbreviations.
    • Good: Mon, Sep 3, 2018
  • When you abbreviate a month or day of the week, capitalize the first letter and do not add a period at the end of the abbreviation.
    • Good: Mon, Sep 3, 2018
  • If you abbreviate, do so for the entire date; don't mix written-out forms with abbreviated forms in the same date.
    • Bad: Mon, September 3, 2018
    • Good: Mon, Sep 3, 2018
  • Be consistent in where you apply abbreviations throughout your documentation; for example, if you choose to abbreviate in table cells, do so in all table cells.
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\b(?:January|February|March|April|May|June|July|August|September|October|November|December),\s\d{4}\bwarningShe was hired in January, 2017.
\b(?:(?:Jan|Feb|Mar|Apr|Jun|Jul|Aug|Sept?|Oct|Nov|Dec)\.\s?\d{1,2}\b|(?:Mon|Tue|Wed|Thu|Fri|Sat|Sun)\.,)warningMon., Sep. 3, 2018
\b(?:Mon|Tue|Wed|Thu|Fri|Sat|Sun),\s(?:January|February|March|April|June|July|August|September|October|November|December)\b|\b(?:Monday|Tuesday|Wednesday|Thursday|Friday|Saturday|Sunday),\s(?:Jan|Feb|Mar|Apr|Jun|Jul|Aug|Sep|Oct|Nov|Dec)\bwarningMon, September 3, 2018

Dates in the middle of a sentence

  • When a MONTH DAY, YEAR date appears in the middle of a sentence, add a comma after the year.
    • Good: The January 19, 2017, release of ...
  • If the date in the middle of the sentence consists of the month and year only, don't use a comma.
    • Good: The January 2017 release of ...
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\b(?:January|February|March|April|May|June|July|August|September|October|November|December) \d{1,2}, \d{4} (?=[a-z])warningThe January 19, 2017 release of the API added new methods.

Why we prefer dates written out

  • Use words to express dates; don't express months as numbers unless you don't have the option.
    • Bad: 02.12.2017
    • Good: February 12, 2017
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\b\d{1,2}/\d{1,2}/(?:\d{4}|\d{2})\b|\b\d{1,2}\.\d{1,2}\.\d{4}\b|\b\d{1,2}-\d{1,2}-\d{4}\bwarningNumeric date order differs by region.The release shipped on 12/02/2017.

Numeric-only date format

  • If you must express a date in numerical date format, use the format YYYY-MM-DD, and separate the elements by using hyphens.
    • Bad: 04/06/2017
    • Good: 2017-04-15
  • If you have a choice of what date to write (such as in a fictional example), choose a calendar day greater than 12 to differentiate it from the month.
    • Bad: 04/06/2017
    • Good: 2017-04-15

Express dates and times together

  • If you must express a date and a time together, mention the date first and then the time.
    • Good: May 4, 2009, at 6 PM

Express divisions of the year

  • Avoid referring to seasons; instead, use the month, quarter, or temperature (if relevant).
    • Bad: Changes are released in the Fall of each year.
    • Good: Changes are released in October of each year.
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\b(?:in|during) (?:the )?(?:spring|summer|fall|autumn|winter)\b|\b(?:in|during) the (?:Summer|Fall|Autumn|Winter)\b|\b(?:summer|winter) months\bsuggestionSeasons differ between hemispheres.Changes are released in the Fall of each year.
