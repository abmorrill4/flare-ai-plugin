Em dashes

  • To indicate a break in the flow of a sentence—or an interruption—use an em dash, also known as a long dash.
  • Don't use an en dash (the shorter dash) or a hyphen in place of an em dash; only use the em dash.

Colons instead of dashes in description lists

  • Don't use an em dash, an en dash, or a hyphen surrounded by spaces to separate an item and its description; instead, use a colon or a period.
    • Bad: Example - This is an example.
    • Good: Example: This is an example.
  • For a series of items and their descriptions, use an HTML description list (<dl>).
    • Good: <dl> <dt>Example</dt> <dd>This is an example.</dd> <dt>Another example</dt> <dd>This is another example.</dd> </dl>
