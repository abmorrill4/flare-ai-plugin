Trademarks

Compiled from the Google developer documentation style guide, Trademarks, licensed CC BY 4.0. Generated: edit the company rules instead, and turn Google rules off or change them there.

Label trademarked terms

  • For trademark marking or attribution in documentation, follow any usage guidelines provided by the owners of the respective marks.

Use trademarks only as modifiers

  • When you use a trademarked term, always use it to modify a noun, not as a noun by itself.
    • Bad: Another option is to use a Chromebook.
    • Good: Another option is to use a Chromebook notebook computer.
  • Don't use a trademark as a verb.
    • Bad: For information about Chromebook computers, google "notebook computers"
  • Never form a possessive from a trademark.
    • Bad: Chromebook's features rely on an internet connection.
  • Never form a plural from a trademark.
  • Never change a trademark in any way.
