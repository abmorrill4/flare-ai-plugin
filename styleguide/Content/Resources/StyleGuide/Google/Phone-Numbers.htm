Phone numbers

Use example phone numbers

  • To show an example phone number, use a US number in the range 800‑555‑0100 through 800‑555‑0199.
  • Never use a real phone number in examples.

Format phone numbers in HTML or Markdown

  • To ensure that a phone number is displayed on the same line, use a nonbreaking hyphen (&#8209;) where appropriate in HTML or Markdown.
    • Good: 415&#8209;555&#8209;0132
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
(?<![\w-])\+?(?:\d{1,3}-)?\d{3}-\d{3}-\d{4}(?![\w-])suggestionKeeps the phone number on one line.Call 800-555-0123 for support.

Format North American phone numbers

  • To format a real phone number in the US, Canada, and other NANP countries, use a nonbreaking hyphen to separate the area code, three-digit exchange code, and four-digit number.
    • Good: 415‑555‑0132
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\(\d{3}\)\s?\d{3}[-‑.]\d{4}\bwarningCall (415) 555-0132 for help.

Format international phone numbers

  • To format a real phone number in non-NANP countries, include the country and area codes.
    • Good: +1‑415‑555‑0132
  • Insert a plus sign immediately before the country code (no space); the plus sign stands in for the exit code.
    • Good: +1‑415‑555‑0132

Format phone numbers that include an extension

  • To specify a phone extension, follow the phone number with the word extension, and then specify the extension number.
    • Good: 415‑555‑0132, extension 987
PatternUseSeverityWhyExample
\d{4},?\s+(?:[Ee]xt\.?|EXT\.?)\s*\d+warningCall 415-555-0132 ext. 987.
