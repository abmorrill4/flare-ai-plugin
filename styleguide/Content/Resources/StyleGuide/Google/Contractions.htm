Contractions

  • Use common two-word contractions such as you're, don't, and there's.

Negation contractions

  • Use negation contractions such as isn't, don't, and can't.
  • If you need to emphasize the negative, use text formatting such as is <em>not</em>, but in most cases don't add emphasis to make your point clear.

Contractions to avoid

  • Don't make up nonstandard contractions such as guides're or browser's (where 's means is).
  • Don't use three-word contractions such as mightn't've.
