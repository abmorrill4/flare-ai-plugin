Timeless documentation

  • Document the current version of a product or feature.
  • Focus on how the product works right now, not on how it has changed from previous versions or how it might change in the future.
  • Avoid time-based words in product documentation that refers to a product's features and capabilities; they're okay only in procedural or time-stamped content such as press releases, blog posts, or release notes.
    • Good: The VM goes offline soon after you send the shutdown command.

Words and phrases to avoid

  • When describing product or feature capabilities, don't use words and phrases that make promises or project plans and strategies, such as at present, as of this writing, or eventually.
  • Don't use words and phrases that are implied by the existence of the documentation itself, such as currently and as of this writing.
    • Bad: The following command-line options aren't currently supported:
    • Good: The following command-line options aren't supported:
  • Avoid words and phrases that become outdated soon after publication, such as soon and latest.
  • If you must use words like new, give a reference point such as a date or version release number.
    • Good: The January 14, 2021 release of BigQuery includes a new resource panel.
  • When describing product or feature capabilities in product and reference documentation, avoid existing.
