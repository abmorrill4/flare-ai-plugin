Introductory phrase preceding colon

  • When a colon introduces a list, make sure the text that precedes the colon can stand alone as a complete sentence.
    • Bad: The fields are:
    • Good: The fields are defined as follows:

Colons within sentences

  • In general, put the first word in the text that follows a colon in lowercase.
    • Good: When you add or update content to an existing project, remember to take these steps: review the style guide, use checklists, enlist a fellow writer or an editor to copyedit your work, and request a developmental edit if you feel that it's warranted.
