New terms

  • When you introduce a new term that you're defining immediately, use italics on the first mention of the term; don't use bold or quotation marks.
    • Good: A Clos network is a kind of multistage circuit switching network.

Words as words

  • When you refer to a word, phrase, or letter in reference to the word, phrase, or letter itself (words as words), use italics; don't use bold or quotation marks.
    • Good: Don't use & (ampersand) as a conjunction. Use the word and instead.
