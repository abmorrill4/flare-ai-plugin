Changes to the Google guide

Rows in this table turn rules from the Google developer documentation style guide off, or change their level, for the whole company. These are the defaults that ship for teams writing Flare help. Each row says why, you see them in Turned-off rules, and you can turn any of them back on there. Timeless-Documentation rules ("currently", "in the future") stay on: turn them off per project for release notes, where Google allows time words.
