# Zorbit FAQ

## Does Zorbit store my command output?

Zorbit keeps the last 30 days of output for each run. After that, the output is removed.

## Can I run a job once?

Yes. Create the job, choose **Run once**, and then click **Save**. See the [quickstart](quickstart.md) for the steps.

## Where can I read about the API?

The reference is at [the developer site](https://example.com/api).

Need more help? Contact the Zorbit support team through the customer portal, and include your account name and the steps you tried.
