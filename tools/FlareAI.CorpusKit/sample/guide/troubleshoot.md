# Troubleshoot Zorbit

Use this page when a job does not run or a worker goes offline.

<details>
<summary>A worker shows the status Offline</summary>

Check that the machine can reach the service on port 443. The worker reconnects on its own when the network returns.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A job never starts</summary>

Open the job and confirm that its schedule is not paused. A paused job appears in grey on the **Jobs** page.

</details>

If you still see an error, read the [FAQ](faq.md) or check the [settings](configure.md).

Need more help? Contact the Zorbit support team through the customer portal, and include your account name and the steps you tried.
